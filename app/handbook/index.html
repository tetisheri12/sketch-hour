How to use Sketch Hour

Everything the app does, tab by tab, plus every keyboard shortcut.

Getting started

Open Sketch Hour with the button at the top of any page. The app has four tabs:

  • Practice: set up and run timed sessions.
  • Library: your boards of references.
  • Timer: a standalone timer for longer pieces.
  • Sources: bring in references from Pinterest, FilmGrab, MapCrunch and your own video files.

Until you add your own references, the Poses, Film scenes and Places boards show generated sample images, so you can try a session straight away.

Practice sessions

Choose what to draw from

Under Draw from, pick one or more boards. Each one shows how many references it holds.

Fixed time or class

Fixed time uses one time for every pose: 30 seconds to 10 minutes, or type your own. Then choose how many poses, or All to use every reference once.

Class follows a plan that starts with short gestures and ends on long studies:

  • Warm-up, 15 minutes: 10 × 30 s, 5 × 1 min, 1 × 5 min
  • Short class, 30 minutes: 10 × 30 s, 5 × 1 min, 2 × 2 min, 1 × 5 min, a 1-minute break, 1 × 10 min
  • Full class, 60 minutes: 10 × 30 s, 5 × 1 min, 5 × 2 min, 2 × 5 min, a 5-minute break, 1 × 10 min, 1 × 15 min

The Schedule strip shows the plan with its total poses and time. If a session needs more poses than your boards hold, references repeat.

Display options

  • Shuffle draws references in random order.
  • Grayscale removes color so you can focus on values.
  • Random mirror flips about half of the poses.
  • Thirds grid lays a 3 × 3 grid over each pose.
  • Sound cues tick in the last three seconds of poses 20 seconds or longer, and chime when the pose changes.

During a session

The controls along the bottom go back a pose, pause and skip ahead. Next to them are grayscale, mirror, grid, sound and full screen. The controls fade when you stop moving the mouse; move it or tap the pose to bring them back.

In the last 10 seconds of poses 30 seconds or longer, the clock changes color. End finishes the session and shows what you drew. Poses you spent at least 5 seconds on count.

Library and boards

Poses, Film scenes and Places are always there. New board adds your own, which you can rename or delete.

Adding references

  • Press Add files, or drag files from your computer onto the Library.
  • Copy an image and press Ctrl+V (⌘+V on a Mac) on any tab. It goes into the board you last opened in the Library.
  • JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF images work, and MP4 or WebM clips up to 20 MB. Large photos are scaled down to 2,400 pixels on the long side to save space.

Browsers don’t let one site save images dragged from another. On other websites, right-click the image, choose Copy image, and paste it into Sketch Hour.

Editing references

Open a reference and use the pencil to change its note, source link or board, or the bin to delete it.

Pinterest

Pinterest boards

The Pinterest boards listed under Sources refresh once a day. They also appear in the Library and under Draw from; press View to open one. Their pins come straight from Pinterest, so you can’t edit or delete them, and a pin removed on Pinterest disappears at the next refresh.

Adding single pins

Open a pin on Pinterest, right-click the image and choose Copy image (on a phone, long-press it). Then click the box under Add single pins and paste. Choose the board it goes into, and add the pin’s link if you want to find it again later.

  • Pasted images always go into your own boards. If a Pinterest board is open in the Library, they go to Poses.
  • The Search Pinterest links open common pose searches on Pinterest.

Reference viewer

Click any reference to open it.

  • Zoom with the scroll wheel, a pinch, a double-click or the zoom buttons. Drag to move around. The zoom label returns to a fitted view.
  • Mirror, grayscale and the thirds grid work as in sessions.
  • Values cycles through Notan (two values, with a slider that moves the split), three values, five values, and back to the image. It works on still images.
  • Frames: for GIFs and clips, step one frame back or forward, pause, and slow playback to ½× or ¼×. Stepping through GIFs needs Chrome or Edge; clips work in every browser.
  • Study timer: pick 2 to 15 minutes at the top and press the timer button. It runs the main Timer.
  • The side buttons and arrow keys move between references on the board.

Some Pinterest pins don’t allow value studies or GIF frame stepping, because Pinterest controls how its images can be used. Copy the pin into one of your own boards to use those tools.

Film stills and street views

FilmGrab

In Sources, open a random film, browse the A–Z list, or search a title. Type the film title for the note, copy a still on FilmGrab, and paste it into the box. It goes into Film scenes.

Frames from your own video files

Under Grab frames from your own video file, open a video, scrub or step frame by frame at the frame rate you choose, and press Grab frame. The frame goes into Film scenes, with the film title and timecode as its note. The video never leaves your device.

MapCrunch street views

Random Street View opens MapCrunch. Turn on Urban for streets and buildings, and Stealth to hide the map and address. Take a screenshot (Win+Shift+S on Windows, ⌘+⇧+4 on a Mac) and paste it into the box to keep it in Places. The perspective study buttons start the Timer for 5 to 30 minutes.

Timer

Pick a preset from 1 minute to an hour, or type minutes and seconds. Set up to 10 rounds with a rest of 15 seconds to 5 minutes between them. +1 min adds a minute while it runs. Turn on Chime to hear when each round ends.

Practice log

The Practice tab shows the minutes you drew over the last seven days, your streak of days in a row, and your poses this week.

  • A session is logged once you’ve drawn for at least 30 seconds.
  • A timer is logged when it finishes, or when you reset it after at least a minute.

Your data

Sketch Hour has no accounts. Your boards, references, settings and practice log are saved in this browser, on this device.

  • Another device or browser starts with only the site’s Pinterest boards. Files you added yourself stay where you added them.
  • Clearing your browser’s site data for Sketch Hour deletes everything.
  • Private or incognito windows may not keep anything after you close them.
