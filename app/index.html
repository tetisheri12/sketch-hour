Free. Nothing to install. No account needed.

Sketch Hour is a practice room for artists who learn from reference.

Collect the poses, faces, film frames and places you want to study, then draw them against the clock. Short poses train you to see the whole figure fast. Long ones give you time to get it right.

Sessions

Practice like you’re in a life-drawing class

Set one time for every pose, or pick a class plan. A class warms you up with 30-second gestures, moves through longer poses and ends on a 10- or 15-minute study, with breaks along the way.

  • Class plans of 15, 30 and 60 minutes
  • Shuffle, grayscale, random mirroring and a thirds grid
  • Sound cues in the last three seconds and at each new pose
Curriculum

A path through the skills of drawing

Thirty units in eight tracks: figure, heads, perspective, light and value, color, composition, animals and environments. Every exercise starts a timed session with references made for it, and your progress is ticked off as you go.

  • A suggested order in ten terms, or pick any track
  • Mannequins, head forms and perspective scenes rendered for practice
  • Master studies from public-domain museum art
Pinterest

Pinterest boards, ready to draw from

Sketch Hour’s Pinterest boards refresh once a day and wait in your Library, ready for a session. Found a pin of your own? Copy it on Pinterest and paste it in.

  • Boards refresh every day
  • Paste single pins straight from Pinterest
  • Drop in your own files, GIFs and short clips
Reference viewer

See the shapes under the detail

Open any reference to zoom in, flip it, switch to grayscale, or reduce it to two, three or five values. Step through GIFs and clips one frame at a time to study movement.

  • Notan view with an adjustable split
  • Thirds grid for checking proportions
  • A study timer right in the viewer
Film stills and places

Study light and space, not only figures

Find stills on FilmGrab and random street views on MapCrunch, paste them in, and give yourself a timed perspective study. You can also grab frames from video files on your computer.

  • Film scenes and Places boards ready to fill
  • Frame-by-frame grabber with timecodes
  • Perspective studies from 5 to 30 minutes
Timer and log

A timer for longer pieces, and a log that adds up

Use the timer for anything outside a pose session, with rounds and rests if you want them. Sessions and timers count toward your minutes this week and your daily streak.

  • Presets from 1 minute to an hour
  • Up to 10 rounds with rests between
  • Keeps the screen awake while it runs, where the browser allows

Keep your hands on the pen

Every control in a session has a key, so you can run a whole session without reaching for the mouse.

  • Space Pause or resume
  • ←→ Previous or next pose
  • G Grayscale
  • F Mirror
  • T Thirds grid
  • V Value study in the viewer

All keyboard shortcuts

Works wherever you draw

Sketch Hour runs in the browser, so there’s nothing to install and nothing to update.

Computer

Keep it in a window beside your drawing app, or go full screen for a session.

Tablet

Use it in split view next to your drawing app. Pinch to zoom into references.

Phone

Prop it up beside your sketchbook and work through a class.
